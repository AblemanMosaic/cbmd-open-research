# AI assistance disclosure

This repository and the research it presents were developed with substantial AI assistance.

AI systems have contributed to source navigation, conjecture generation, mathematical drafting, finite-model implementation, test construction, interface design, package verification, and editorial preparation. The human author remains responsible for deciding what is proposed publicly, for checking citations and permissions, and for responding to mathematical criticism.

Executable agreement, hash verification, and fluent exposition are not substitutes for specialist review. Each discovery therefore distinguishes written argument, exact model evidence, unresolved transport obligations, external review, and novelty assessment.
