## Adopted into CBMD

- possibility, specification, selection, lowering, realization, evidence, and revision are distinct stages;
- one research question and claim identity may have multiple proof, computation, replay, and review realizations;
- every target declares preservation, known loss, fallback, and refusal instead of pretending implementations are identical;
- request, intent, terminal result, claim-promotion decision, and revision have separate roots;
- observations and evidence may motivate a successor contract but cannot rewrite their own governing contract;
- host observations remain outside semantic execution roots;
- unsupported capability and uncertain execution fail closed with typed refusals;
- self-check, separate implementation, and external review are separate independence levels.

## Deliberate CBMD differences

CBMD's axes describe research and verification realizations, not visual or world-engine controls. Mathematical object identity, hypotheses, source boundaries, and claim ceilings are preserved invariants. A compiled statement, finite computation, proof term, source correspondence, cold replay, and review-ready packet remain different evidence classes.

The current contract is a local executable candidate. It grants no novelty, publication, release, reviewer approval, or independent-verification standing.
