# 0.3.0-candidate

This candidate revision records an answer to open question 6, in part.

- A written derivation from published theorems shows that the sixteen-tower
  statement at `Γ ∈ {1, 2}` (`g = 2`, `b ≥ 3`) follows from Heegaard–Floer
  theory. The steps:
  1. ECH, Seiberg–Witten Floer homology and Heegaard–Floer homology agree as
     `U`-modules.
  2. `Y_b` is surgery on the Borromean knot `B_2`.
  3. `CFK^∞(B_2)` has zero differential, by a rank count.
  4. The large-surgery theorem applies from `2g − 1 = 3`.
  5. So `HF^+` is sixteen towers in every torsion spin-c structure.
- Consequently the tower count is not a new invariant. The dossier's proposed
  contribution narrows to what this route does not give: the raw cap classes
  as filtered bases, the explicit low-sector recurrence with its off-diagonal
  junction, and the action-filtered information.
- Each step is sourced in `review/PRIOR_ART.md`. The gaps are listed there
  too: Step 1 rests on a cited summary of the isomorphisms, Step 3 on a quoted
  knot-complex description, and Step 2 is argued rather than quoted.

This is the opposite direction from the error recorded in
`docs/CORRECTIONS.md` for this dossier. That note wrongly said the count was
already *published*. This revision says it is *derivable* from published
results, gives the derivation, and makes no publication claim.

No claim state was promoted. Questions 1–5, specialist review,
proof-assistant verification and novelty of the filtered formula remain open.
