## The result under review

The local Lean development connects the pinned source's integer entry to an explicit master numerator under its orbit and mismatch hypotheses. Exact recurrences and a shared finite convolution evaluate that numerator. Zero branches outside the support extend the construction to every joint-matrix cell.

A final equivalence characterizes swap-orbit membership coordinate by coordinate: unselected slots remain fixed, and each selected head/slot pair is unchanged or swapped. This permits a constructive decision in place of exhaustive Boolean-function search while preserving the entire joint matrix.

BCM-1 is the arithmetic cost model used for the recurrence core. For `d = R-1-f`, its counted semantics gives exactly `(3d+2, 2d, d+1)` multiplications, exact divisions, and additions. For `f < R`, these are bounded by `(3R-1, 2R-2, R)`.
