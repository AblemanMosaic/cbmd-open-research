# Prior art

- **S. Hopkins, T. McConville and J. Propp, *Sorting via chip-firing*, EJC
  24(3) (2017).** They prove sorting for even `n`, and for loops at the origin
  only (Theorem 20). They state Conjecture 21 (`ℤ⟨S⟩`; Remark 22 relates its
  two end conditions to a balance condition on `S`), Conjecture 24 (`rℤ`),
  Conjecture 25 (`r(ℤ⟨S⟩)`), Conjecture 26 (checked for `n ≤ 9`) and
  Conjecture 27 (limited simulations, "around .33"). The paper gives no
  computational evidence for Conjectures 21, 24 or 25, and does not discuss a
  converse of Conjecture 21.
- **C. Klivans and P. Liscio, *Confluence in labeled chip-firing*
  (arXiv:2006.12324).** They prove Conjecture 24 (Theorem 3.2). They also prove
  the all-loops case (a loop at every vertex, `n ≡ 3 (mod 4)`, Theorem 3.13),
  which is exactly the `S = ℤ` case of Conjecture 21, and discuss Conjecture
  25 in the all-loops and parallel-edge setting (§3.6). Our reading of the
  arXiv text finds no treatment of an arbitrary loop set `S`. They do not
  address Conjectures 26 and 27.
- A 2026 literature search found no later resolution of Conjecture 21 for
  general `S`. This is a search result, not a novelty determination.

Not a novelty determination.
