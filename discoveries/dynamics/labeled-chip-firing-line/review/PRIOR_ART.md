# Prior art

- **S. Hopkins, T. McConville and J. Propp, *Sorting via chip-firing*, EJC
  24(3) (2017).** They prove sorting for even `n`. They state Conjecture 26
  (checked for `n ≤ 9`) and Conjecture 27 (limited simulations, "around
  .33").
- **C. Klivans and P. Liscio, *Confluence in labeled chip-firing* (2020).**
  They resolve HMP Conjectures 24 and 25 and the self-loop case, and show that
  sorting fails for odd `n`. They do not address Conjectures 26 and 27.

Not a novelty determination.
