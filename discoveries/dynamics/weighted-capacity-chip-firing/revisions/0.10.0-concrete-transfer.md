# Revision 0.10.0 — the weighted transfer theorem, fully concrete

Since 0.1.0, `weighted_transfer` has taken the firing function as a parameter.
It has also taken the ordinary theory as two explicit hypotheses: termination,
and uniqueness of ordinary stabilization. The claim record carried the nonclaim
"ordinary well-foundedness and uniqueness enter as explicit hypotheses and are
not proved here". The unit-edge predecessor
(`CBMD-DYN-CAPACITY-FIRING-001`) had discharged both for its own update. This
revision does the same for multigraphs (`Concrete.lean`).

| declaration | content |
|---|---|
| `wFire` | the concrete update: the fired vertex loses `outdegree u`, every other `v` gains `mult u v` |
| `wFire_wellFounded` | ordinary firing terminates on an ordered multigraph: each firing decreases the chip vector lexicographically in topological order |
| `wFire_commutes`, `wFire_locally_joinable` | two unstable vertices fire in either order to the same state |
| `newman_terminal_unique` | the terminal form of Newman's lemma |
| `wFire_unique_stabilization` | a reachable ordinarily stable state exists and is unique |
| **`weighted_transfer_concrete`** | the weighted transfer theorem with the concrete update and **no ordinary hypothesis** |

Axioms: `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`.

The hypotheses left are the structural ones: the order, positive outdegree,
`mult u v ≤ incomingBound v`, and `outdegree v + incomingBound v ≤ capacity v + 1`.
As in the predecessor, `outdegree` is a parameter. The theorem holds for any
positive choice, and it is the caller's job to supply the true one, the
nonsink multiplicities plus the sink's. The predecessor's checked decoder
(its revision 0.2.0) shows how to certify that for concrete instances.

The arbitrary-digraph characterization (revision 0.9.0) is stated for
terminal stability, which needs no ordinary hypothesis. For cyclic graphs
transfer fails anyway (revision 0.2.0).
