# Revision 0.6.0 — a stale open item, retired with a citable corollary

Since revision 0.1.0 this dossier has listed as unresolved *whether the bound is
necessary at receivers of incoming multiplicity one*. It has been settled since
revision 0.4.0; nothing recorded that.

At a receiver `v` fed along a unit arc, the bound `d(v) + m(u,v) ≤ C(v) + 1`
reads `C(v) ≥ d(v)`.

- **Sufficient** by the transfer theorem, since 0.1.0.
- **Necessary** by `bound_violation_defeats_transfer` (0.4.0) at `m(u,v) = 1`:
  for a live sender `u`, `C(v) < d(v)` is exactly the violated hypothesis
  `capacity v + 1 < outdegree v + mult u v`. That theorem never requires the
  receiver to be live, so the case of a live sender aimed at a receiver that can
  never fire is covered as well.

`bound_necessary_at_unit_arc` states the instance by name so it can be cited;
it depends on `[propext, Quot.sound]`. The witness is the 0.4.0 one at unit
multiplicity: the sender full, the receiver at its own capacity — below its
threshold, so stable — and everything else empty.

No new mathematics. The point of the revision is that the open-items list was
out of date for two revisions, and an out-of-date open list misdirects anyone
choosing what to work on.
