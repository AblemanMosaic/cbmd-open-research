# Revision 0.4.0 — necessity proved, and the one remaining gap named

Revision 0.3.0 established the three live-set conditions as sufficient and
exact-by-census. This turns the census into proof wherever proof is available,
and states precisely what is left.

## Necessity of the bound

If a live `u` sends to a live `v` with `capacity v + 1 < outdegree v + mult u v`,
load `u` to its capacity and give `v` exactly `capacity v + 1 - mult u v`.
Then `v` is left strictly below its own threshold — which is exactly what the
violated bound permits — while already holding enough that `u`'s bundle
overflows it. Every other vertex is empty. The state is capacity-terminal and
not ordinarily stable.

`bound_violation_defeats_transfer` compiles; it depends on `[propext, Quot.sound]`.
The truncated-subtraction branch, where the bundle alone exceeds the capacity,
is handled rather than assumed away.

## Necessity of live-closure

Already available: `liveToDeadFilledSet` builds the two-member filled set from a
live vertex with an arc to a dead one, and `filled_set_obstruction` turns it
into the failure. It depends on no axioms.

## The conditions exclude every obstruction of this shape

`ordered_live_closed_excludes_filled_set` proves that ordering plus live-closure
admit **no** `FilledSet` at all: from the live witness, live-closure keeps the
successor chain live and the ordering strictly raises its rank, which a finite
vertex type cannot sustain. It depends on `[propext]` alone.

So the sufficiency proof and the obstruction are not two unrelated facts; the
conditions are precisely strong enough to rule the obstruction out.

## What is still not proved

Turning "no rank function exists on the live subgraph" into a concrete directed
cycle. That is the classical finite-digraph statement that an acyclic finite
relation admits a topological order. It is not specific to this model, it is not
proved here, and it is the **sole** remaining gap between the compiled theorems
and the exhaustive census.

For context rather than excuse: the predecessor theorem takes its ordering as a
hypothesis in exactly the same way (`edge_order : TopologicallyOrdered edge` in
`CBMDFiniteDynamics.FullTheorem`). The rank hypothesis used here is at the same
standard, so this is a gap shared with the existing result, not one introduced
by the generalization. Closing it would strengthen both.
