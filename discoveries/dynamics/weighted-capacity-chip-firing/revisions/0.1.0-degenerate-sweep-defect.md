# Search defect caught before registration

The first sweep lowered the capacity bound at **every** vertex simultaneously to
test sharpness. At a source vertex that makes `C(u) < d(u)`, so the vertex can
never fire and the dynamics freeze. The sweep therefore reported zero
nonconfluent systems below the bound — for the wrong reason, and it would have
been read as "the bound is not tight".

The defect was caught by a positive control: the search was first required to
reproduce the predecessor dossier's known multiplicity-two adverse witness at
`C(v) = d(v)`. It did not, which exposed the frozen regime.

`verification/reference/search_bound.py` lowers capacity at one receiver at a
time, keeps every vertex able to fire, and carries the positive control as a
permanent part of its output. No claim was registered from the defective sweep.
