# Revision 0.5.0 — the remaining gap closed

Revision 0.4.0 proved necessity of two of the three live-set conditions and
named one step it could not prove: turning *"no rank function exists on the live
subgraph"* into a concrete obstruction. That step was the classical
finite-digraph fact that an acyclic finite relation admits a topological order.

It is now closed, not by proving that classical fact, but by **not needing it**.

## The move

The ordering condition was carried into Lean as three hypotheses — a rank
`Vertex n → Nat`, a bound `rank v < n`, and strict increase along live arcs. All
three exist only to supply a descent measure. Replace them by well-foundedness
of the relation that the descent actually runs on:

```lean
def LiveArc (mult outdegree capacity) (v u) : Prop :=
  Live outdegree capacity u ∧ Live outdegree capacity v ∧ 0 < mult u v
```

`LiveArc v u` holds when `u` sends to `v` and both are live, so `Acc LiveArc x`
says that chasing blocked senders forward from `x` terminates. This is the right
object: the sufficiency proof was already a well-founded recursion on it, with
`n - rank` standing in as a measure.

## What this buys

**Sufficiency generalizes.** `wf_terminal_implies_ordinary_stable` drops the
rank, the bound `rank v < n`, and with them every use of finiteness. The old
statement is recovered as an instance: `wellFounded_of_rank` derives
`WellFounded LiveArc` from the 0.3.0 hypotheses, so
`live_terminal_implies_ordinary_stable` is now a corollary rather than a
separate result.

**The gap closes.** `filledSetOfNotWellFounded` builds the obstruction directly
from failure of the hypothesis. Take membership to be *"not accessible"*. From
an inaccessible vertex there is always an arc to an inaccessible vertex —
otherwise `Acc.intro` would make it accessible — and that choice is exactly the
successor map a `FilledSet` requires. A vertex that is not live is accessible
for free, since every `LiveArc y x` carries `Live x`, so the witness is live
with no extra hypothesis. No vertex is counted anywhere: no pigeonhole, no
topological order, no finiteness.

The two directions combine:

```lean
theorem wellFounded_iff_no_filled_set
    (live_closed : ∀ u v, Live outdegree capacity u → 0 < mult u v →
      Live outdegree capacity v) :
    WellFounded (LiveArc mult outdegree capacity) ↔
      (FilledSet mult outdegree capacity → False)
```

`live_closed` is load-bearing in the reverse direction and not decoration: a
live vertex pointing at a dead one is a `FilledSet` while `LiveArc` stays well
founded, which the census finds 413,604 times.

## Honest accounting of what is still imported

The classical finite-digraph fact has not been proved; it has been moved out of
the theorem. What remains unproved is that *a finite live subgraph with no
directed cycle is well founded* — the statement that lets the exhaustive census
**decide** the hypothesis by cycle detection. That is a property of the decision
procedure, not a step inside any theorem here. The converse direction, which is
what necessity needs, **is** proved: `not_wellFounded_of_live_cycle` turns a
closed cycle of live vertices into a `FilledSet` and so into failure.

## Evidence

`verification/reference/equivalence.py` checks the two statements against the
same 798,025-system box as the 0.3.0 census, by methods chosen not to share
machinery with `characterize.py`:

- well-foundedness by Kahn peeling, not the DFS colouring the census predicate
  uses;
- `FilledSet` existence by a greatest-fixpoint kernel, which has no counterpart
  in the census at all;
- the conclusion of the theorem simulated directly — every capacity-reachable
  capacity-terminal state tested for ordinary stability. That is weaker than
  TRANSFER, and it is deliberately what is measured: checking TRANSFER would not
  test what Lean proves.

| quantity | count |
|---|---|
| systems in the box | 798,025 |
| hypotheses satisfied | 119,251 |
| conclusion fails under the hypotheses (**unsound**) | **0** |
| conclusion holds where the hypotheses fail (**incomplete**) | **0** |
| `WellFounded` holds, conclusion fails | 418,602 |
| `WellFounded` fails, conclusion **holds** | **0** |
| `WellFounded` fails, conclusion fails | 260,172 |
| theorem B agrees under `live_closed` | 244,453 (0 disagreements) |
| theorem B disagrees without `live_closed` | 413,604 |

Two of these rows are content gates rather than statistics.

*`WellFounded` fails ⇒ conclusion fails, with no exception.* This is not an
observation the census was fishing for; it is what 0.5.0 **predicts**, by
composing `filledSetOfNotWellFounded` with `filled_set_obstruction`. An occupied
cell there would refute the revision rather than weaken it.

*`WellFounded` holds and the conclusion fails, 418,602 times.* Without this the
other two conditions would be idle passengers and the gate would be measuring
nothing. The script makes that explicit rather than leaving it to inference:
each hypothesis is replaced by `True` in turn, and each replacement produces
unsoundness. A 0-unsound headline for a conjunction whose parts can be deleted
freely would be evidence of nothing, and three defects earlier in this dossier
were exactly of that kind.

The 0-unsound/0-incomplete pair also sharpens the 0.3.0 result: the census there
showed the three conditions exact for TRANSFER; they are exact for the theorem's
own conclusion too.

`Classical.choice` is genuinely used, and not hidden: the successor map of the
obstruction is a choice function over "there is an arc to another inaccessible
vertex". That is the price of building a witness from the *failure* of a
hypothesis, and it is the standard price.
