# Revision 0.8.0 — the last imported fact, proved

Revision 0.5.0 reformulated the ordering condition as well-foundedness of
`LiveArc` and recorded one classical fact as still imported: that a *finite
acyclic* live subgraph is well founded. It was not a step inside any theorem.
It was what licensed the census to **decide** the hypothesis by cycle
detection, which is what both `characterize.py` (DFS colouring) and
`equivalence.py` (Kahn peeling) do.

It is now compiled (`FiniteAcyclic.lean`, Mathlib-free):

| declaration | content | axioms |
|---|---|---|
| `wellFounded_of_acyclic` | on `Fin n`, `(∀ x, ¬ TransGen R x x) → WellFounded R` | `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound` |
| `acyclic_of_wellFounded` | any well-founded relation has no `TransGen` cycle | none |
| `liveArc_wellFounded_iff_acyclic` | `WellFounded LiveArc ↔ LiveArc has no cycle` | `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound` |

**Proof.** The measure is the number of `R`-ancestors of a vertex, counted over
`List.finRange n`. If `R y x`, every ancestor of `y` is an ancestor of `x`, and
`y` is an ancestor of `x` but — by acyclicity — not of itself, so the count
drops strictly. `Classical.choice` enters only to decide `TransGen` inside the
count.

## Correspondence with the census

`TransGen LiveArc x x` is a directed cycle through live vertices along arcs of
positive multiplicity, since `LiveArc` requires both endpoints live. That is
exactly what `acyclic_on(a, n, live)` in `characterize.py` and the Kahn peeling
in `equivalence.py` test. No new census is needed; the existing agreement of
those two procedures over 798,025 systems (revision 0.5.0) now sits on a
compiled equivalence rather than an imported fact.

## Standing

Every step of the ordering half of the characterization — sufficiency,
obstruction, and the decision procedure — is now compiled. What remains a
census result is the exhaustiveness of the full three-condition predicate
over the enumerated box, as before. The unresolved list loses its last
mathematical item; novelty and external review remain.
