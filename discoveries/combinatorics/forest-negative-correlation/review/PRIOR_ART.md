# Prior art

- **J. Kahn (2000), and G. Grimmett and S. Winkler, *Negative association in
  uniform forests and connected graphs*, Random Struct. Algorithms 24 (2004),
  arXiv math/0302185.**
  - Their Conjecture 1.1 says that the uniform forest and the uniform
    connected subgraph are edge-negatively-associated. Questions (1.6) and
    (1.7) ask for the weighted forms.
  - For forests, they verified negative correlation by computer for all
    simple graphs on at most 8 vertices, and on 9 vertices with at most 18
    edges.
  - For connected subgraphs, they report no direct computation. They obtain
    only planar-dual consequences of the forest result.
  - They note that multigraphs are "more interesting".
- **C. Semple and D. Welsh, *Negative correlation in graphs and matroids*, CPC
  17 (2008).** They cite the Grimmett–Winkler ranges. They state that positive
  evidence for the connected-subgraph conjecture is limited, and they report no
  new graph computations.
- **D. G. Wagner (2008), Cocks, Stark, Huang, Tang–Zhang, and Erickson.**
  These give structural and partial results: Rayleigh matroids, the 2-sum, the
  series–parallel class, large `K_n`, and the large-fugacity limit. They are
  cited in the two 2026 papers below.
- **arXiv:2609.26134 (2026)** proves negative correlation of uniform-forest
  edges on `K_n` for every `n`. In the arXiv text we read, its introduction
  cites only Grimmett–Winkler for computational verification.
- **R. A. Çiçeksiz and M. Ravichandran, arXiv:2610.00549 (2026).** They give
  large-fugacity coefficient formulas and prove the `q < 1` random-cluster
  case on `K_n`. They also report "exact coefficientwise computations"
  supporting the graphical conjectures, but the arXiv text we read does not
  say which graphs or what range.

This dossier extends the published exhaustive range to all simple graphs on 9
vertices for forests and on at most 9 vertices for connected subgraphs, in the
weighted (all `λ > 0`) form, and to two multigraph families. The 2026
computation in arXiv:2610.00549 may overlap; it is unspecified. This is not a
novelty determination.
