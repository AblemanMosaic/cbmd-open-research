# Revision 0.5.0 — `SOURCE_BOUND_COMPILED` withdrawn

Revision 0.3.0 added `SOURCE_BOUND_COMPILED` to this dossier's standing. It
should not have.

`docs/CLAIM_STATES.md` defines the status as: *the stated theorem compiles
against the declared concrete formal model and pinned toolchain without
admitted proof escapes.* The declared model of this claim is the packed source
definitions. The Lean development of revisions 0.3.0 and 0.4.0 compiles U1 and
the class-count injectivity against an **abstract** model of its own, in which
"a nonzero source row lies in the target's orbit" is a **hypothesis** rather
than a consequence of the source definitions. Its two hypotheses are measured
against this dossier's finite model, which is good evidence that the abstract
theorem applies to that model. It is not a source-bound proof.

The dossier's own unresolved list said so throughout: *source-bound formal
proof* and *formal correspondence to the exact packed source definitions* were
never removed, and the promotion contradicted them. The maintainers apply the
same standard elsewhere: the parity-amplitude dossier has Lean compiled against
the actual source definitions, and still withholds this status pending
checkout replay.

What stands unchanged: the theorems compile, their axioms are recorded, the
bridge checks pass, and the census gates hold. What changes is only the label,
which now says no more than the evidence does.
