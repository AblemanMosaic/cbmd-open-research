| 0.4.0 | **Theorem M**: for `γ_1` faithful and `γ_2` *any* representation, `|K(γ_1 ⊕ γ_2)| ≥ |K(γ_1)|`, with equality iff `γ_2` is a multiple of the trivial representation. Proved via a new route: Lemma A identifies `|K(γ)|` with the weighted count of arborescences of the McKay quiver rooted at the trivial vertex, after conjugating `nI − M` by the diagonal matrix of irreducible degrees to make it a digraph Laplacian with weights `M_ij δ_j / δ_i`; Proposition B shows the count rooted at `χ_v` is `δ_v²` times it and so re-proves the order formula (F3) by elementary linear algebra. Corollary M1 is the reduction Gaetz's Question 2 needs; Corollary M2 is superadditivity, which is tight. Lemma 2 and the sector lemma are kept, marked superseded for this purpose.
