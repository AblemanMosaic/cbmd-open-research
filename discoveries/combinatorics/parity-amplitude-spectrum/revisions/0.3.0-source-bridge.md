# 0.3.0 candidate — source bridge decomposition

The guarded native closed form is not yet proved, but its source-correspondence bridge is now decomposed into five compiled, no-escape facts:

- orbit mismatches are exactly the active coordinates omitted by the witness swap set;
- mismatches are contained in the active set;
- two swap sets produce the same native state exactly when they agree on active coordinates;
- `integerDifference` is the exact signed sum over subwords;
- native integer delta between orbit states depends only on active-coordinate swap agreement.

These facts remove the color-specific ambiguity left by the binary finite audit. They reduce the remaining theorem to signed cancellation for inactive coordinates followed by a finite powerset count. They do not themselves prove the guarded `entry_closed_form`, centered vanishing, or novelty.
