# Revision 0.6.0 — compiled parity reflection

The previously admitted reflection step has been separated into a no-escape Lean module and compiled under the pinned Lean 4.34.1 workbench.

The proof realizes the binomial-basis interpolant as a rational polynomial of degree `< R`, proves its values agree with the integer interpolant, and forces reflection by equality on the `R` consecutive interpolation nodes. The integer-valued reflection theorem then follows by evaluation and exact casting.

This revision does **not** prove the native centered-vanishing corollary. That composition with the guarded native `entry_closed_form` remains the next obligation. It also does not establish clean-checkout replay, independent verification, or novelty.
