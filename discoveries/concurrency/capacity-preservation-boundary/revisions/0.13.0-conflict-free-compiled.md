# Revision 0.13.0 — the conflict-free characterization, compiled

Revision 0.12.0 decided preservation for conflict-free nets by a written
argument. The characterization behind it is now compiled
(`ConflictFree.lean`, Mathlib-free, no `sorry`):

```lean
theorem not_preserves_iff_certificate (cf : IsConflictFree N) (hin : HasInput N) :
    ¬ Preserves N cap ↔ Certificate N cap
```

`Certificate N cap` is: some `t0` with `pre t0 ≤ cap`, and a set `R ∋ t0` such
that every `t ∈ R` has an *option* — an output place `q` and a value `v ≤ cap q`
above `t0`'s floor at which `t` overflows — that either **ends** (no consumer,
the consumer is `t` or `t0`, the consumer has another input, or the consumer's
lower literal holds at `v`) or whose consumer is again in `R`.

| declaration | content | axioms |
|---|---|---|
| `certificate_of_bad` | completeness: the transitions ordinarily enabled at a bad marking form a certificate | `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound` |
| `end_unique` | following the chosen options from `t0`, the visited transitions are linearly ordered, so at most one of them ends | `Classical.choice` |
| `bad_of_certificate` | soundness: raise only the visited choices; every transition is blocked | `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound` |
| `not_preserves_iff_certificate` | the equivalence | `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound` |

## Where the hypotheses enter, in the compiled proof

* **Conflict-freeness** makes the step relation deterministic (`step_det`):
  the consumer of the chosen place is unique. This is what turns "follow the
  options" into a single chain, and what makes `end_unique` true.
* **Every transition has an input place** is used exactly once, in
  `unvisited_blocked`: a transition off the chain is shown to have an input at
  its floor or below. Without it, an input-less transition is ordinarily
  enabled for free and nothing blocks it — the failure the census measured on
  1,061 of 5,000 nets that have one.

## What is not compiled

That the greatest-fixpoint procedure of revision 0.12.0 decides
`Certificate`: `Certificate` asks for *some* post-fixpoint containing `t0`, and
the greatest fixpoint is the union of all of them (Knaster–Tarski on a finite
lattice). That step is classical and is bridged here by census rather than
proved: `verification/conflict_free.py` transcribes `Certificate` literally and
decides it by brute force over **every** candidate set `R` and **every** value
`v`, with no thresholds and no fixpoint, and it agrees with the procedure on
all 20,000 nets (gate `bridge_lean_certificate_matches_procedure`).
