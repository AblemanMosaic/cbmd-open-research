# Revision 0.14.0 — the input-place hypothesis is exactly what makes conflict-free nets tractable

Revision 0.12.0 left open conflict-free nets **with** input-less transitions,
where its procedure was measurably wrong (1,061 of 5,000 random nets). They are
coNP-hard, already with every capacity 1 and transitions of at most two inputs.

## The construction

For a CNF on `n` variables, with places `x⁺_i`, `x⁻_i` of capacity 1:

| transition | inputs | outputs | blocked exactly when |
|---|---|---|---|
| one per variable | `x⁺_i, x⁻_i` | — | not both hold |
| one per clause | **none** | its literal places (`x⁺_a` for `a`, `x⁻_a` for `¬a`) | some literal place holds |

Each place is consumed only by its variable's transition, so the net is
conflict-free. A capacity-dead marking is exactly a consistent partial
assignment under which every clause has a true literal. The clause
transitions have no inputs, so they are always ordinarily enabled, and the
"some transition is enabled" half of a bad marking is free.

## Compiled (`InputLess.lean`)

| declaration | content |
|---|---|
| `inet_conflictFree` | the net is conflict-free |
| `satisfiable_of_capDead` | any admissible capacity-dead marking yields a satisfying assignment |
| **`inputless_preserves_iff_unsatisfiable`** | for at least one clause, `Preserves (inet G) icap ↔ ¬ Satisfiable G` |

Axioms at most `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`.

The "at least one clause" hypothesis is stated rather than hidden. With no
clauses the net has no input-less transition and preserves, although the
empty CNF is satisfiable. The census checks that exception as stated.

The last row turns the random failure count of 0.12.0 into a structured
witness. The procedure is wrong on exactly the unsatisfiable instances,
because it never treats the clause transitions as obligated.

## Where the line now sits

| class | status |
|---|---|
| conflict-free, every transition with an input | polynomial (0.12.0; characterization compiled 0.13.0) |
| conflict-free, input-less transitions allowed | **coNP-complete** (reduction compiled) |
| one conflict place, every transition with an input | coNP-complete (0.10.0) |

Two structural features each suffice for hardness on their own: a single
conflict place, or input-less transitions. Without either, the problem is
polynomial.
