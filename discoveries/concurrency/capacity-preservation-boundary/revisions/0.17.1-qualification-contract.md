# Revision 0.17.1 — fail-closed Lean-backed gates

This revision changes the qualification contract, not the mathematical
statement or its evidence.

`CAPACITY_PRESERVATION_LEAN_DECIDER` and
`CAPACITY_PRESERVATION_CROSS_DECIDER` were marked optional. Consequently their
failures, including failure caused by a missing Lean toolchain, did not fail
aggregate full qualification. Both gates are now required. The
`--repository-only` scope remains the supported dependency-free route.

No theorem, finite count, source file, or retained evidence receipt changed.
